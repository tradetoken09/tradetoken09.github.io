Trade Token — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Trade Token ("TT", "we", "our", or the "App") may receive information made available through Pi SDK authentication, such as a Pi username or other information necessary to authenticate your Pi account.

We may also process information related to your use of Trade Token, including mining activity, mining sessions, TT balances, transaction-related records, Trust Score or reputation-related information, and referral or participation information where applicable.

We do not request or store your Pi wallet passphrase, private keys, or secret recovery information.

  • Provide user support
